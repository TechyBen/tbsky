# skytreeone privacy

skytreeone collects nothing.

- It has no login and never asks for or touches your Bluesky account.
- It sends nothing anywhere: no analytics, no tracking, no servers of its own.
- The forest runs entirely inside your browser from files bundled with the extension.
- It only runs on `bsky.app`. It doesn't read your posts, messages or feed; it only changes how the page's backgrounds look.
- Your settings (sound, volume, scene length and so on) are kept by your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which your browser may sync between your own devices if you're signed in to it.
- The sun, moon and stars use an approximate location estimated on your device from its time zone. It never leaves your device.

If a future version reads public Bluesky activity (for example, live posts appearing in the forest), this page will be updated before that ships.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/TechyBen/tbsky/issues.
